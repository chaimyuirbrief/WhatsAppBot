*📋 GROUP RULES — please read*

*BE CIVIL*
• Treat everyone here with respect. No insults, no harassment, no personal attacks.
• Disagree with the point, not the person.

*STAY ON TOPIC*
• Keep posts relevant to what this group is for.
• Take long side conversations to a DM.

*NO SPAM*
• No advertising, referral links, chain messages or repeated posting.
• Don't tag everyone unless it genuinely concerns everyone.

*PRIVACY*
• Don't share anyone's phone number, address, photo or private messages without their say-so.
• What's posted here stays here.

*ADMINS*
• Admins may remove messages or members that break these rules.
• If you think something was removed in error, message an admin — don't relitigate it in the group.

_Send *#rules* at any time to see this again._
